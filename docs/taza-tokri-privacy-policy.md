## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
When you create an account, log in, or place a grocery order, we may collect:
- **Full Name**: Used to address you and identify recipient packages.
- **Phone Number**: Used for SMS One-Time Password (OTP) authentication and courier communication during delivery.
- **Delivery Addresses**: House number, street name, area, landmark, and delivery instructions needed for doorstep delivery.
- **Order Details**: Items purchased, delivery slot preference, and chosen payment method (e.g. Cash on Delivery).

### B. Location Information
- **Approximate & Precise Location**: With your explicit permission, we access your device's location to:
  - Verify whether your area is within our active delivery zones.
  - Automatically detect your nearest mandi fulfillment hub.
  - Enable accurate doorstep drop-offs and real-time courier tracking.
- Location data is collected only while using the application and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

### C. Media & Photos (Optional)
- With your permission, the app may access camera or photos strictly for taking produce quality audit pictures, proof of delivery, or customer service dispute resolution.

### D. Device and Technical Information
- Diagnostic data, crash logs, device model, operating system version, and unique identifiers collected through Firebase to ensure application reliability and performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for:
1. Processing, packing, and delivering your fresh grocery orders.
2. Authenticating your identity securely via SMS verification.
3. Sending critical order updates, delivery status alerts, and service notifications.
4. Providing real-time live tracking of assigned delivery couriers.
5. Responding to customer support and inquiry requests.
6. Improving app performance, security, and preventing fraudulent transactions.
