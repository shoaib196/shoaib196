## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Balancr** ("we", "our", or "us"). Balancr helps individuals, roommates, and groups track shared expenses, split bills, and manage personal budgets. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your financial records remain private and secure.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Data**: Name, email address, or phone number used to create your profile and identify you to group members.
- **Group & Expense Data**: Transaction amounts, categories, descriptions, split shares, currency selections, and group memberships.
- **Settlement Records**: Logs of recorded payments and debt settlements between members.

### B. Device & Technical Information
- Diagnostic data, network connection status, and error logs used to manage offline sync and ensure data consistency.

### C. Camera & Photos (Optional)
- With your permission, the app accesses your camera or gallery strictly for scanning bill receipts or setting group icons. Receipt images remain private to your account or group.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
1. Calculate shared group balances and debt simplifications.
2. Synchronize your financial transactions across your devices and between group members in real time.
3. Manage offline caching so your expense tracking works without internet access.
4. Deliver notifications regarding newly added group expenses or settlements.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
We do not sell, rent, or monetize your financial or personal data. We only interface with essential cloud infrastructure (e.g. secure AWS EC2 PostgreSQL storage) required to run the real-time sync engine.

---

## 5. Data Security
- All network communications between the mobile app and our servers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS and Secure WebSockets (WSS).
- Local expense caches are protected on your device.

---

## 6. Data Deletion
Users have the full right to delete their data:
- You can delete any transaction, group, or account data directly in the application.
- To permanently delete your entire profile and all associated data, you can contact us at `shoaibslamat152@gmail.com`.
